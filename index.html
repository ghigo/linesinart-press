Boilerplate copy

Copy/paste descriptions for articles, event pages, and newsletters.

1–2 sentences

Lines in Art is a children's picture book that invites kids to explore real artworks through looking, noticing, and conversation—building visual thinking and creativity through simple prompts and playful observation.

~50 words

Lines in Art helps young children build the habit of looking closely at art. Instead of "explaining" paintings, the book offers simple prompts that encourage kids to notice details, make connections, and share what they see—turning museum-looking into an at-home, read-together experience for families and classrooms.
